1. Introduction

Silver Expostores ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website silver.expostores.com and related sites.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Pre-order Form: Full name, email address, phone number, city, and source of discovery
  • Contact Information: When you reach out to us via email or WhatsApp
  • Payment Information: Processed through secure payment gateways (not stored by us)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Cookies & Tracking: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, time spent, device information
  • Analytics Data: User behavior, conversion paths, referral sources
  • Pixel Data: For retargeting and audience analysis (Meta Pixel)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Process pre-order requests and send confirmation emails with discount codes
  • Send product updates and promotional offers (with your consent)
  • Analyze site performance and user behavior through Google Analytics
  • Create targeted advertising audiences (Meta Pixel)
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience.

6. Data Security

  • We use HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Access to databases is restricted and password-protected
  • Payment information is processed through PCI-compliant gateways
  • We do not store credit card details
  • Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest

7. Data Retention

  • Pre-order Data: Retained for 2 years unless deletion is requested
  • Email Records: Kept for order fulfillment and support (up to 2 years)
  • Analytics Data: Automatically aggregated after 24 months
  • Cookies: Vary by type (essential: session, non-essential: up to 2 years)

8. Your Rights

GDPR (European Users)

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to withdraw consent

CCPA (California Users)

  • Right to know what data is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of data sales
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: privacy@expostores.com

9. Cookie Consent & Preferences

When you first visit our site, a cookie consent banner will appear. You can:

  • Accept All: Enable all cookies and tracking
  • Reject Non-Essential: Only essential cookies are enabled
  • Manage Preferences: Choose which cookies to allow

You can change your preferences anytime by clicking the cookie settings button in the banner.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately and contact you.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of changes.

12. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Consent: You've given explicit permission (cookie banner)
  • Contract: Necessary for pre-order fulfillment
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with laws
  • Legitimate Interest: Improving our website and services

Your data may be transferred to, and processed in, countries outside your country of residence. These countries may not have data protection laws as comprehensive as your own. By providing your information, you consent to such transfers.
